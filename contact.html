    How to reach the newsroom

    The fastest route to the right person is email. For anything editorial, corrections, story tips, pitches, letters for publication, write to editor@cypruspulse.com with a subject line that says what the message is. We aim to answer editorial mail within two working days, and correction reports jump the queue: an error on this site is our problem before it is anyone else's.

    If you prefer paper or a phone call, our details are below. The office address and numbers shown are placeholders to be replaced before launch; the email addresses are the durable channel.

    • Editorial: editor@cypruspulse.com
    • Advertising & partnerships: ads@cypruspulse.com

    • Post: Cyprus Pulse, [STREET], Nicosia 1010, Cyprus
    • Phone: +357 22 [XXX XXX] (weekdays, 09:00 to 17:00 EET/EEST)

    Advertise with us

    Cyprus Pulse reaches an audience most Cyprus advertising misses: English-speaking residents, relocated professionals, international students, investors and frequent visitors who make real decisions about where to live, bank, study and spend. Our display advertising runs through the Google AdSense and Google Display Network programmes, which means standard formats, brand-safety controls and measurement your media buyer already understands.

    For direct sponsorships, newsletter placements in The Pulse Briefing or partnership ideas beyond programmatic display, email ads@cypruspulse.com with your goals and timeline; we respond within one working day with our current media information. Two rules never bend: sponsored material is always clearly labelled, and advertisers never see, shape or veto editorial content before publication. That separation is what makes the audience worth reaching.
